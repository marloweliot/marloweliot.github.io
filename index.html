Projects

2025–26

Self-supervised behaviour discovery Dissertation · 88/100

Python · PyTorch · HDBSCAN · UMAP · scikit-learn

An end-to-end pipeline that finds behavioural states in 7.4 million frames of 3D movement data from 52 tracked bumblebees, with no manual labelling. A convolutional autoencoder learns compact representations of movement, and HDBSCAN clusters them into behaviours.

A systematic architecture search cut reconstruction loss to about 1.5% of the baseline. It also showed that better reconstruction does not guarantee more stable clusters.

2025

Evolving flocking behaviour

Python · PyBullet · evolutionary algorithms · NumPy

Rebuilt Reynolds' boids flocking model in a PyBullet physics simulation, with randomly placed obstacles so evolved behaviour could not overfit to one layout. Benchmarked four evolutionary algorithms (hill climber, microbial GA, spatial GA and island-model GA) on tuning the flock's parameters.

Proposed a stratified model that gives agents on the edge of the flock different rules from those inside it, challenging Reynolds' assumption that every agent behaves identically. It outperformed the standard model under all four algorithms.
